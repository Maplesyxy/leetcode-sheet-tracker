LeetCode Sheet Tracker

Tracking turns on automatically whenever a leetcode.com tab is open. Nothing to toggle.

1. Connect your Google Sheet

  1. Open (or create) the Google Sheet you want to log to.
  2. In the sheet, go to Extensions → Apps Script. Delete the sample code.
  3. Copy the script below (button on the right) and paste it into the editor. Save (⌘S).
  4. Click Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy and authorize when asked (click "Advanced → Go to … (unsafe)" if Google warns; it's your own script).
  6. Copy the Web app URL (ends in /exec) and paste it below, then press Test connection.

2. Session behavior

A "session" starts when you open a problem and ends after this much inactivity or when you close the tabs. Its total length and what you solved get written to the Sessions sheet.

3. What gets written

  • Problems — one row per problem, updated on every Submit: status, attempts, attempts-to-solve, total minutes, first/solved/last dates, plus the latest submission's result, pass/fail, language, tests passed, runtime, memory and submission id.
  • Sessions — one row per sitting: start, end, duration, active minutes, problems attempted/solved, topics covered.
  • Weak Areas — one row per topic ranked by a struggle score (unsolved share, attempts per problem) with a verdict and the unsolved problems to revisit.
If the sheet is unreachable, rows are kept locally and retried every few minutes. "Run" (test) executions are not logged, only real Submits.
